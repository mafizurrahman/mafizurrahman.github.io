About

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering (expected 2028) at Prairie View A&M University, advised by Prof. Lijun Qian. I earned my M.Sc. in Computer Science from the same university (CGPA 4.0/4.0) and my B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from East West University, Dhaka. My research sits at the intersection of hardware-aware AI, analog in-memory computing, and multi-agent language models, with the goal of making AI systems more efficient, reliable, and interpretable.

My work spans hardware–software co-design for LLM inference, transformer robustness under analog hardware non-idealities, LLM routing, and higher-order attention. I have collaborated with Sandia National Laboratories on analog neural network inference and with researchers at the University of Southern California on sparse higher-order attention and interpretability.
